## `src/beta/src/export/web.rs` chapter markers — the comment "Like the video export"

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-164, finding R-20261127T070609Z#N2)

The finding is accurate. The comment says that the player keeps the
chapters like the video export. The first filter is the same (a finite time
in `0..=duration`). But `chapter_metadata` in `src/beta/src/export/video.rs`
then drops a chapter that ends at or before its start, thus a chapter at the
duration is a marker in the player and is not in the MP4.

The fix is prose. It changes a comment and no behavior. The code of both
filters is correct, the test `a_chapter_at_the_duration_is_dropped` holds
the MP4 rule, and `src/beta/README.md` states the difference. A new comment
is new text that a fresh review reads, and the loop then starts one more fix
round for a comment.

The boundary: this settles the wording of the comment. A change of the web
filter that drops a chapter that the MP4 keeps, or that keeps a chapter
outside `0..=duration`, is a new finding.
