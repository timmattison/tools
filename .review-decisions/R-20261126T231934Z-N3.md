The finding is accurate. The test feeds `"x\x1b[?2".chars().skip(1)`, thus the
`x` does nothing. The name speaks of two chunks, but the test feeds one
`Scanner` one char at a time, with no chunk.

pending between calls of `feed`, and that the sequence completes on a later
call. A mutant that drops the pending state between calls fails it. Thus only a
reader of the test gets a wrong picture, and no defect of the code gets past
the test.
