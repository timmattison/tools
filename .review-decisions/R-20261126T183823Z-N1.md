## `src/crap/src/main.rs` `unfolded_alias_of` — two macOS tests take their precondition from the volume of TMPDIR

**Settled** 2026-11-26 (branch issue-314, finding R-20261126T183823Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. A probe on 2026-11-26 made the symlink of
`unfolded_alias_of` in two directories:

- Under the default TMPDIR of macOS (`/var/folders/...`, on the data volume),
  `realpath` keeps the `/System/Volumes/Data` prefix. The two names give two
  canonical paths, and the two tests do their work.
- Under a directory on `/Volumes/SamsungSSDs`, `realpath` removes the prefix.
  The two names give one canonical path, and the `assert_ne!` of the helper
  stops both tests.

**The fix costs more than the defect in this round.** The defect occurs only
when a person sets TMPDIR to a volume other than the data volume. macOS does not
set TMPDIR so, and the macos-latest runner of CI does not. The probe session on
this machine had its TMPDIR under `/var/folders`.

When the defect occurs, the failure is loud and names its cause. The test stops
at a precondition with the message "realpath must keep two spellings of one
home, else this test proves nothing". The failure is a false red, never a false
green, thus it cannot hide a defect of `crap`.

The fix is a change of test code, for example `tempfile::tempdir_in` on a
directory of the data volume. This round of the review loop is record-only. A
change of test code starts one more review cycle.

**The boundary.** This record settles only a TMPDIR that a person sets to a
volume other than the data volume. Each of these occurrences reopens it:

- a default TMPDIR of macOS, or the TMPDIR of a CI runner, on which the two
  tests fail
- a hook, a runner, or a script of this repository that sets TMPDIR to a
  different volume
- a different test that takes its precondition from the volume of TMPDIR in the
  same way
