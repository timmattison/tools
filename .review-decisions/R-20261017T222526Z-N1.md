## Commit order on an unpushed branch — the expansion tests of `ha` came after their green commit

**Settled** 2026-10-18 (branch issue-565, finding R-20261017T222526Z#N1)

The review found that the green commit `6485a6a2` added two behaviors of the board
of `ha` with no red test for them: the rule that an expansion goes away when its run
leaves the board (`self.expanded.retain(|id, _| shown.contains(id));` in `settle`),
and the error text of `fail_jobs`. Their tests landed in `4e07c7f2`, the commit after
it, and its body says that they pass at once. The TDD rule in `CLAUDE.md` asks for the
red commit first. The finding is accurate.

It is not fixed, because the defect is the order of two commits and nothing else.

**The coverage is real.** The body of `4e07c7f2` records that a mutation that
disables the settle step fails four of its tests. The run of `address-issues` for this
finding did the two mutations again on `921f0abc`, with `cargo test -p ha --lib
board::tests`:

- With no `retain` line in `settle`, four tests fail:
  `a_run_that_replace_drops_loses_its_expansion`,
  `a_repository_that_keep_only_drops_loses_its_expansion`,
  `a_run_that_leaves_the_window_loses_its_expansion`, and
  `a_run_that_becomes_other_loses_its_expansion`.
- With a `fail_jobs` that changes nothing, two tests fail:
  `a_failed_fetch_after_an_answer_keeps_the_jobs_and_adds_the_error` and
  `a_failed_fetch_before_any_answer_shows_the_error_only`. The third,
  `a_later_answer_clears_the_error`, still passes, because it tests that `set_jobs`
  removes the error.

What TDD gives, a test that can fail for the behavioral reason, is present. Only the
order of the two commits is wrong.

**The one fix is a rewrite of 21 commits, and it buys nothing durable.** The decision
`R-20260820T153740Z-N2` says that a rebase is free on an unpushed branch. On this
branch it is not. A swap of `6485a6a2` and `4e07c7f2` gives every later commit a new
SHA. The review `R-20261017T222526Z` ends on `53ffac8`, which is one of those
commits. After the swap, `pr-merge-gate.ts` finds that commit is not on the branch,
calls the review `rewritten`, and refuses the merge until a fresh review of the whole
branch. Against that: this repository squash-merges every PR with an explicit
`--subject` and `--body`, so no commit of this branch reaches `main`. Tim chose to
decline when he saw both costs.

**The boundary.** This decision settles the order of `6485a6a2` and `4e07c7f2` on the
branch `issue-565` only, where the tests exist and a mutation shows that they can
fail. It does not settle a missing test, or a test that nobody showed can fail. It
does not change the rule of `R-20260820T153740Z-N2` for an unpushed branch where no
review names the commits that a rebase moves. It does not permit implementation
first as a habit.
