## `src/nle/src/preview.rs` `Preview` — the doc names the local `budget`, and `new` does not state its precondition on the duration

**Settled** 2026-11-26 (branch issue-151, finding R-20261127T022554Z#N5)

The finding is accurate on both points:

- The doc of `Preview` names `budget`, which is a local of `Preview::new`, not
  a constant or a field.
- The bound of `2 * budget + 1` snapshots holds only when `recording.duration`
  is at or past the time of the last event. With a duration of 0 and events
  over 10,000 s, the interval is 1 s, and the time axis takes up to 10,000
  snapshots.

The app is safe. The pipeline sets the end of the timeline to
`end.max(last_time(&result))` in `src/nle/src/pipeline.rs`, thus each recording
that the app gives to `Preview::new` has a duration at or past its last event.
No path of the app breaks the precondition.

**The fix costs more than the defect in this round.** The first fix is prose:
a doc comment that names the precondition and calls `budget` the spacing budget
of `new`. Prose fixes nothing. The second fix sizes the interval from the
larger of the duration and the time of the last event. That fix is new code and
needs a red test. This round of the review loop is record-only. Each fix is a
diff that a review must read, and that review can find a new nit in it. Then
one more fix round starts.

**The boundary.** This record settles only the doc of `Preview` and the
unstated precondition of `Preview::new`. Each of these occurrences reopens it:

- a caller of `Preview::new` that can give a recording whose duration is before
  its last event
- a change of the pipeline that stops the end of the timeline at the time of the
  last event
