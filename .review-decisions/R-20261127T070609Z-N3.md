## `src/nle/src/pipeline.rs` `FilterFn` step 9 — the rule for a filter that adds or removes events

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-164, finding R-20261127T070609Z#N3)

The finding is accurate. Step 9 of the `FilterFn` doc says that, when a
filter adds or removes events, a chapter keeps its place in proportion to
the last time of the region. `move_chapter` scales a chapter up to the last
seen time by the last answered time over the last seen time, and a later
chapter moves by the shift. The two texts agree only for a span filter,
where the last seen time is the end of the region.

The fix is prose. It changes a doc comment and no behavior. The doc of
`move_chapter`, in the same file, states the rule that the code obeys. A new
step 9 is new text that a
fresh review reads, and the loop then starts one more fix round for a doc
comment.

The boundary: this settles the wording of step 9 at `78bd494f8`. A change
of `move_chapter` that makes its own doc wrong, or a filter that depends on
the rule of step 9 and moves a chapter to a wrong time, is a new finding.
