## `specs/2026-11-26-nle-timeline-research.md` — the drag rules give only the principle of a drag, and the parts of issue #150 decide the detail of each kind of drag

**Settled** 2026-11-26 (branch issue-159, finding R-20261126T231729Z#I1, decision H-159-2)

The finding is accurate. `Edit::TrimClip` can make a clip wider, and the new
area then shows source time that no tile covers. Thus the principle of rule 7,
"during a drag, only the style of the clip changes", and rule 3, "Dioxus skips
each tile during a drag", do not tell an implementer what that new area shows
during the drag.

The fix asked the spec to divide each trim by its direction, and to count the
tile index from a new origin. Three review rounds in a row found a new gap in
the detail of one drag, and each fix gave the next round more text to read. On
2026-11-26, Tim answered the stall hold H-159-2 of issue-fleet on issue #159.
The spec keeps only the principle: during a drag, only the style of the clip
changes, and the drop gives one edit to the engine. Commits 32ca3c7 and 745c88c
removed the list of what each edge drag changes. Rule 7 now says that the parts
of issue #150, after its split, decide that detail.

The detail does not belong in a research document. The parts of issue #150
write the drag code, and their tests can show what each drag draws. A rule in
the spec cannot.

**The boundary.** A finding about the detail of one kind of drag is this
decision. Such detail is what a move, a trim of either edge, or a trim that
makes a clip wider or narrower changes during the drag. It is also which
element moves the tiles, which tiles render, and which `Edit` the drop applies.
Each of these occurrences reopens it:

- the spec states such detail again, in a rule, in the results, or in the
  limits of the measurements
- a statement of the spec about a drag that a measurement contradicts, for
  example the move of a clip, which the driver measured
- a principle of rule 7 that no kind of drag can obey
