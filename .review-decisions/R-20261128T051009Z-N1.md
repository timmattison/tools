## `src/nle/src/filters/keystroke_hud.rs` — a cell that the HUD writes again loses its combining marks, and the limit names only the label

**Settled** 2026-11-28 (branch issue-161, finding R-20261128T051009Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. `put_char` of `termstate` gives a character of no
width no cell, thus a cell keeps only its base character. The repair of a
pending wrap (`restore` in `src/nle/src/filters/keystroke_hud/paint.rs`) prints
`cell.ch` of the cell under the cursor again. A row that ends in e + U+0301 in
the last column thus shows é before a box paints, and e after the repair. The
cells under a box lose their marks the same way when the box goes away. The
limit in `README.md` and in the module doc of `keystroke_hud.rs` names only the
label.

**The fix costs more than the defect.** The fix that the finding asks for is
prose in two files: the README entry of `nle` and the module doc. This round of
the review loop is record-only. Two changed documents are new text that a
review must read, and that review can start one more fix round. The fix that
removes the defect is larger: `termstate` must keep the combining marks of each
cell, which changes its `Cell` type and each reader of that type.

The defect is a loss of a mark on the cells that the HUD writes again. The base
character, the style and the position of each such cell stay correct.

**The boundary.** This record settles only the loss of combining marks on the
cells that the HUD writes again from `termstate`, and the text of the limit.
These occurrences reopen it:

- a cell that the HUD writes again with a wrong base character, a wrong style,
  or at a wrong position
- a loss of a combining mark on a cell that the HUD does not write
- a change of `termstate` that keeps combining marks, after which the repair
  must print them
