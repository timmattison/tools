## `README.md` — "each chapter" gets a marker on the timeline

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-164, finding R-20261127T215657Z#N2)

The finding is accurate. `README.md:6626` says "A blue line marks the time of
each chapter", and `README.md:6798-6799` say "The timeline shows a marker at
the time of each chapter". `ChapterRow::of` draws no marker for a chapter
outside the window of the timeline. Such a chapter can be after the end of the
project, and the chapter panel keeps it with its after-the-end note.

The words that the finding gives, "each chapter inside the project", are not
exact either. Finding `R-20261127T220921Z#N1` shows that a chapter after the
end of a short project, or of a project with no clip, is inside the window and
gets a marker. The exact rule is "each chapter inside the window". A reader
who does not know the window gets no help from that word. A reader who looks
for a late chapter finds it in the chapter panel, with its note.

**The fix costs more than the defect.** The fix is a change of README prose in
two places. It changes no behavior, and a fresh review reads the changed text.
The loop then starts one more fix round for a word that has no exact
replacement a user can read.

**The boundary.** This record settles only the words "each chapter" in the
two README places. These occurrences reopen it:

- a chapter inside the window that gets no marker
- a marker at a time that is not the time of its chapter
- a chapter that the chapter panel does not show
