## `src/nle/src/pipeline.rs` `FilterRegistry::run` — the in-place arm and the span arm spell the length of the region in two ways

**Settled** 2026-11-26 (branch issue-168, finding R-20261126T231605Z#N2)

The finding is accurate. In the `match filter` of `FilterRegistry::run`, the
in-place arm computes `top = region_end.min(*end).max(start)` and the length
`top - start`. The span arm computes
`(region_end.min(*end) - start).max(0.0)`. These are two spellings of one rule.
`RegionView::length` and step 1 of `SpanFilterFn` state that rule.

The two expressions give the same value for each finite `start`, `region_end`,
and `end`. They also agree when `region_end` or `end` is infinite, for example
for `FilterRegion::All`, because both then give infinity. Thus no caller sees
two different lengths for one region today.

before the match. It changes no behavior. This round of the review loop is

**The boundary.** This record settles only the two spellings of the length in
