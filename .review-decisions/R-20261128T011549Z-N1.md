## `src/nle/src/ui/tests.rs` `each_style_selector` and `style_selectors` — two parsers of the selectors of `STYLE` stay

**Settled** 2026-11-28 (branch issue-166, finding R-20261128T011549Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. The merge 3c0ef18b brought `style_selectors` from
`main`, and it renamed the helper of this branch to `each_style_selector`. Both
helpers split `STYLE` at `}` and `{`. Only `each_style_selector` removes the
comments and splits at commas. The two names do not tell a reader which helper
does what.

The two helpers give different shapes to different tests.
`the_dismiss_button_keeps_its_own_hover_color` needs the rule preludes in
source order, and `style_selectors` gives a `Vec` in that order.
`each_control_of_a_field_has_a_rule_of_the_style_sheet` needs a set of single
selectors, and `each_style_selector` gives a `BTreeSet`. Each test passes now.
This branch adds no caller of `style_selectors`, thus decision
`R-20261127T193502Z-N1.md` stays closed.

**The fix costs more than the defect.** A rename or a merge of the two helpers
changes no behavior of a test. It is a change of a test file, a review reads
that change, and the change thus starts one more review cycle. This round of
the review loop is record-only.

**The boundary.** This record settles only the two helpers as they are now.
Each of these occurrences reopens it:

- a third parser of the selectors of `STYLE` in the tests of `nle`
- a new caller of `style_selectors` on this branch
- a comment before a selector in `src/nle/assets/main.css` that one of the two
  helpers reads as a selector
