## `src/faulte/src/history.rs:1025` — the doc of `bytes_used` says that it counts each container

**Settled** 2026-11-24 (branch issue-669, finding R-20261124T215117Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. The doc of `History::bytes_used` says that it counts
"each container by its capacity". The field `accounts` (`history.rs:762`) is
a `render::Accounts`, which holds a `HashMap<Uid, String>` of the names of the
accounts. `bytes_used` (`history.rs:1029-1034`) counts the entries, the rows,
the text of the gaps, and the identities. It does not count `accounts`.

The memory that the doc leaves out is small and has a limit. The map holds
one name for each account that owns a process of a frame. A Mac has some
tens of accounts, thus the map holds some kilobytes at most, against a budget
of 64 MiB. The map does not grow with the count of frames.

The fix is a change of the doc, or a new term in `bytes_used` with a new
test. The round that found it was record-only. Either change is new text
that the next review must read, and that review starts one more round. The
round costs more than a doc that leaves out some kilobytes.

The boundary: this settles the omission of `accounts` from the doc and from
the count. A container of the history that grows with the count of frames,
and that `bytes_used` does not count, is a new finding.
