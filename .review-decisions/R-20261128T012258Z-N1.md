## `src/nle/src/filters/color_scheme/tests.rs` — the `38;5;0;31` case of `a_cube_operand_is_not_read_as_a_color_code` reads only the prefix `38;2;`

**Settled** 2026-11-28 (branch issue-166, finding R-20261128T012258Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. The third assertion of the test holds only
`starts_with("38;2;")`. A mutant of `Converter::rewrite` that converts the
`38;5;0` group and then drops the `31`, or keeps it as written, gives the same
prefix and passes. No other test of `rewrite` converts a group after a
converted `38;5` group.

The purpose of the test is the operand rule: the `0` after `38;5` is the index
of a color, not a reset. The prefix is sufficient for that rule. If the
converter reads the `0` as a reset, the output starts with `38;5;` or with a
reset, not with `38;2;`. The `1;38;5;31;32` case compares the whole string,
thus a loss of the group after an operand that the converter does not change
fails that case.

**The fix costs more than the defect in this round.** The fix compares the
whole output of the `38;5;0;31` case. It is a change of a test file. A review
reads that change, thus the fix starts one more review cycle, and this round
of the review loop is record-only.
