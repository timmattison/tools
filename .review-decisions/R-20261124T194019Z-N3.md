## `src/nle/frontend/src/App.css` — the error banner stays in the implicit fourth row

**Settled** 2026-11-24 (branch 170, finding R-20261124T194019Z#N3)

`.error-banner` has `grid-column: 1 / -1` and no row. The three named rows of
`.app-layout` (menu, preview and properties, timeline) are full. Thus the grid
puts the banner in an implicit fourth row below the timeline, and a load error
shows at the bottom of the window, far from the Open button.

The finding is accurate. It is not fixed, because:

- **Only the position is wrong.** The banner shows the error with
  `role="alert"`, and its Dismiss button operates.
- **No test can hold the position at this time.** The frontend tests run in
  jsdom (`src/nle/frontend/vite.config.ts`), and jsdom computes no layout. A
  fix of the CSS has no red test.
- **The layout is a scaffold.** The preview pane and the timeline are
  placeholders for issues #151 and #150, and those issues change the grid.
- **The round is record-only.** A fix is new code, and it starts a new review
  cycle.

The boundary: a change to `grid-template-areas` of `.app-layout`, or a second
banner, reopens this decision.
