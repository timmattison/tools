## `src/nle/src/format.rs` `parse_clock` — the text `-0` gives `Seconds(-0.0)`

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-164, finding R-20261127T070609Z#N5)

The finding is accurate. The plain form of `parse_clock` reads `-0` as
`-0.0`, and `Seconds::new` accepts it, because `-0.0 >= 0.0` is true. The
project file then holds `-0.0`, and Edit puts the text `-0` into the Time
field.

No time is wrong. `-0.0 == 0.0` is true, `format_clock` shows `0:00.0`,
and the chapter stays at the start of the recording. Two results are
visible. Edit puts the text `-0` into the Time field. `chapters_in_order`
and the render sort with `total_cmp`, which puts `-0.0` before `0.0`, thus
a `-0` chapter comes first among the chapters at time zero, whatever
their ids are.

The fix costs more than the defect. It is a red and a green commit in
`parse_clock` or in `Seconds::new`, and a fresh review reads both. The loop
then starts one more fix round for a sign that changes no time.

The boundary: this settles `-0` and other spellings of negative zero. A
negative time that is not zero and that `parse_clock` or `Seconds::new`
accepts, or a place where `-0.0` gives a different time than `0.0`, is a
new finding. The order among chapters at time zero is part of this
decision.
