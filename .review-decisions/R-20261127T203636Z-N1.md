## `src/beta/src/export/video.rs` `DROPPED_CHAPTER_RULE` — the rule text keeps "at least 1 ms"

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-164, finding R-20261127T203636Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. `chapter_metadata` rounds each chapter time and the
duration to whole milliseconds with `to_millis`. Then it drops a chapter whose
start is not before the start of the next chapter or before the end. Thus the
real rule is "the same whole millisecond", not "less than 1 ms". Chapters at
3.0004 s and 3.0006 s are 0.2 ms apart, and both stay (3000 and 3001). Two
chapters that are almost 1 ms apart can round to one millisecond, and one of
them drops.

The text in `DROPPED_CHAPTER_RULE`, in `chapter_report`, and in
`src/beta/README.md` tells a user why the export dropped a chapter. The error
of that text is less than 1 ms in each direction. "1 ms" still gives the user
the correct size of the gap, and the time base of the MP4 chapters is 1/1000.

**The fix costs more than the defect.** The fix changes the text of a report
line, the test strings that hold that text, and the README. That text is new
text that a fresh review reads. The loop then starts one more fix round for a
gap of less than 1 ms.

**The boundary.** This record settles only the words of the rule. These
occurrences reopen it:

- a change to `chapter_metadata` that drops a chapter whose rounded start is
  before the rounded start of the next chapter and before the rounded end
- a change of the time base from 1/1000 to a different unit, with no change of
  the text
- a report whose count of dropped chapters does not agree with the count that
  `chapter_metadata` drops
