## `src/termstate` — RIS keeps the scrolling region (reported again)

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-165, finding R-20261127T201651Z#N2)

The finding is accurate, and it is the same defect as finding
R-20261127T195703Z#N1 (record `R-20261127T195703Z-N1.md`). A later review
reported it again with a new id. The RIS arm (`src/termstate/src/lib.rs:1995-2015`)
does not reset `scroll_top` and `scroll_bottom`, and the RIS arm of `bf33efbb`
has the same gap. On a screen of 80 columns and 24 rows, after `ESC [ 2 ; 4 r`
and `ESC c`, `scroll_region()` gives `(1, 3)`. xterm gives the full screen.

The finding adds one fact: rounds 3 and 5 of this branch made more rules read
the region. A line feed scrolls only at the bottom margin, and CUD and CUU stop
at the margins. Thus a stale region changes more output than on `main`. The
defect itself is the reset that RIS does not do, and that code is older than
this branch. Thus the defect is a margin defect older than this branch, as
`.review-decisions/2026-11-27-issue-165-termstate-scrolling-region-follow-up.md`
defines it. This commit adds this finding id to entry 1 of the list of that
record.
