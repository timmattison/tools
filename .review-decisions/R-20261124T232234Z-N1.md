## `src/msgs/src/state.rs` — `AppState::export` keeps the database lock while it copies the attachments

**Settled** 2026-11-24 (branch msgs, finding R-20261124T232234Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. `AppState::export` takes the lock of `self.db` and
keeps it through `export_conversation_range`, which copies each attachment with
`std::fs::copy` (`src/msgs/src/export.rs`). During a large export,
`list_conversations`, `get_messages`, `get_message_position`,
`search_messages`, and `check_db_access` wait for the lock. The commands are
`#[tauri::command(async)]`, thus the window stays responsive. But a click on a
different chat or a search shows nothing until the export ends.

**The fix costs more than the defect.** A fix splits the export into a read
under the lock and a copy without the lock. That change touches the signature
of `export_conversation_range` and its tests. The defect is a delay, not a
wrong result: each command that waits gets its answer when the export ends. An
export is also an action that the user starts and watches. This round of the
review loop is record-only, thus the split starts one more review cycle.

**The boundary.** This record settles the wait of the other commands during an
export only. A lock that stays held after an export ends or fails reopens this
record. A command that gets a wrong answer after the wait, or a window that
stops responding, also reopens it.
