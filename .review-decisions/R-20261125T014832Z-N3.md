## `src/faulte/src/tui/view.rs:602` — a selected past gap shows the header lines of a span of 1 m or wider above the reason of the gap

**Settled** 2026-11-25 (branch issue-669, finding R-20261125T014832Z#N3)

The finding is accurate. With a span of 1 m or wider, `App::refresh` calls
`history.span(Past(gap_id), span)` for a selected gap, and `span` gives the
reading of the frames before the gap. `draw` paints the header lines of that
reading. `paint_table` (`view.rs:396`) then fills the table area with only the
reason of the gap. With one frame, `reading()` is `None`, and the screen shows
no header lines.

Each header line is true. It states the processes with faults, the measured
time, the swap, and Claude Code for the span that ends at the selected gap.
The screen shows no false number. It leaves out the rows that those lines
count, and it shows the reason of the gap in their place.

The finding itself asks for a discussion of two fixes: show the rows of the
span below the reason, or leave out the header lines when a gap is selected.
Both are a change of the view and a new test. The round that found it was
record-only, and that code is new code that the next review must read. That
review starts one more round, and the round costs more than header lines that
are true for the span that the person selected.

The boundary: this settles the header lines of a span above a selected gap.
A header line that states a number that is false for its span is a new
finding.
