## `src/faulte/tests/history-budget.rs:80-83` — the doc of `SMALL_FRAMES` names the whole budget as the cause

**Settled** 2026-11-25 (branch issue-669, finding R-20261125T131316Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. The doc of `SMALL_FRAMES` came with the red test
43d0778a. At that commit, the whole budget was the only rule that evicted an
entry. The fixes 8c690f34 and 7f80288b added `ENTRY_SHARE_DIVISOR`
(`src/faulte/src/history.rs`). The deque of the entries now stops at an eighth
of the budget, and that share rule makes the first frame leave long before the
entries reach the whole budget. The sentence stays true as arithmetic, but the
cause that it names no longer acts first.

The defect is in a comment of a test, and it changes no behavior. The test
`worst_cases_stay_inside_the_budget` and its assertions still hold the rule:
400,000 frames with no row fill the deque many times, and the bytes stay
inside the budget. A fix changes only prose. That change is new text that a
review must read, and it starts a new review cycle for a comment that
misleads nobody about what the test proves.

The boundary: this settles the wording of the doc of `SMALL_FRAMES` on branch
issue-669. A change of the value of `SMALL_FRAMES` that no longer fills the
deque past its share, or an assertion that depends on the whole budget as the
cause of the eviction, is a new finding.
