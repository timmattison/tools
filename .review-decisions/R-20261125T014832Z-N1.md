there tells of a defect in `release` or in the eviction, and the panic hook
of `altscreen` restores the terminal before the message prints.

  workspace lint set and the crate do not turn on `clippy::expect_used`, so
  the expectation is never fulfilled. An unfulfilled expectation is a warning,
