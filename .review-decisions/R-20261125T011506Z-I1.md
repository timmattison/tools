## `.husky/pre-commit` — the SIGPIPE fault of `grep -q` under `pipefail` moves to issue #760

**Settled** 2026-11-25 (branch ic-emit-rgba, finding R-20261125T011506Z#I1)

The finding is correct, and the defect must be fixed. Under `set -o pipefail`, each trigger that sends `git diff` through a pipe into `grep -q` can be false on a large staged list: grep exits at its first match, `git diff` dies with SIGPIPE (status 141), and the block does not run. A probe on this branch at `ea4af21b` gave 0 cargo calls for `README.md` plus 6000 more files, and 0 for `Cargo.lock` plus 6000 more files, and the hook exited 0 each time.

This is not a decision that the fix costs more than the defect. Tim decided (decision `H-745-4` on issue #745) that issue #760 fixes it, and this branch does not. The defect is older than this branch, and `main` has it too (lines 45 and 105 of `.husky/pre-commit` at `5242d374`). Issue #760 gives the probe, each of the four triggers of this branch, and the fix.

The boundary: until issue #760 closes, a finding that a trigger of `.husky/pre-commit` sends a pipe into `grep -q` under `pipefail` is this finding again. A new trigger of that shape that this branch adds after `ea4af21b` is a new occurrence.
