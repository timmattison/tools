## `src/gsw/src/push.rs` `SKIP_VERB` — the hint of the `P` question is cut at 80 columns

**Settled** 2026-10-26 (branch issue-610, finding R-20261026T141811Z#N6)

The review measured the row of the `P` question for the branch `issue-610`. The
update row is 108 columns, and the create row is 115 columns. The rows of `p` are 69
and 76 columns. `PushUi::overlay` cuts the row with `truncate_right`. Thus at 80
columns the hint `[y/Enter = push without hook, n/Esc = cancel]` is cut, and
`n/Esc = cancel` does not show. The finding is accurate.

It is not fixed, because no shorter wording makes the create row fit, and the part
of the row that carries the warning already shows.

**The measurements.** For the branch `issue-610`:

| Wording | Update row | Create row |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Now (the wording of issue #610) | 108 | 115 |
| The hint says `push` | 95 | 102 |
| `, no pre-push hook?` and a hint of `push` | 87 | 94 |
| `, no hook?` and a hint of `push` | 78 | 85 |

Each option cuts the create row at 80 columns. Only the last option fits the update
row, and it changes the question of issue #610 to a less clear one.

**The warning is in the question, not in the hint.** `truncate_right` cuts the end of
the row, and the question comes first. At 80 columns the question shows in full:
`Push 3 commits to origin/issue-610 without the pre-push hook?` is 61 columns, and
the create question is 68 columns. Both rows are yellow. The hint repeats what the
question says.

**`p` has the same limit.** The row of `p` also loses its hint when the branch name
is long. A branch name of 20 characters puts the update row of `p` at 80 columns. A
rule that the hint always shows is a change for `p` and `P` together, for example a
second row for the hint. It is not a change of the words of `P`.

**The owner chose this wording.** Tim read these measurements during the run of
`address-issues` on 2026-10-26 and kept the wording of issue #610.

**The boundary.** This decision settles the length of the words of the `P` question
and its hint only. It does not settle a question whose own text is cut at a common
width, so that the warning does not show. It does not settle a pane where the row of
`p` loses its hint with a short branch name.
