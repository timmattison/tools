## `src/claude-transcript/src/lib.rs` `is_valid_session_id` — the doc no longer says why the shape is exact

**Settled** 2026-11-19 (branch issue-674, finding R-20261119T155102Z#N5)

The finding is accurate. In `crap`, the doc of `is_valid_session_id` said that the exact UUID shape keeps each path separator, traversal sequence, and shell metacharacter out of the file lookup and the shell function. The move into `claude-transcript` kept the rule but not that reason. The doc now says only "a canonical UUID, 8-4-4-4-12 hex digits, either case". `crap` still joins the id into paths and into its shell function, and `chf` joins it into `<uuid>.jsonl`.

**Why it is not fixed.**

- The fix is prose: one sentence in a doc comment. Prose does not stop a person who loosens the rule. A test does.
- The shared crate has that test. `valid_session_id_rejects_traversal_and_separators` in `src/claude-transcript/tests/transcript.rs:198` fails when the rule lets `..`, `/` or `\` through. `crap` keeps its own copy of the test (`src/crap/src/main.rs:3786`). Thus the part of the finding that says only the tests of `crap` hold the rule is not correct.
- The review loop of this branch is in its record-only round. A doc change goes into the next incremental review, and the sweep of nits starts again.

**The boundary.** This record settles the missing sentence in the doc of `is_valid_session_id`. It does not settle a change that loosens the rule, or the removal of either test. A real change of either kind reopens this decision.
