## `README.md` and `src/nle/src/filters/keystroke_hud.rs` — the limit of a cut string sequence names OSC and DCS only

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-161, finding R-20261127T044020Z#N1)

The finding is correct. vte 0.15 puts `ESC X`, `ESC ^` and `ESC _` into its
`SosPmApcString` state, and only CAN, SUB or ESC ends that state. Thus an
SOS, a PM or an APC that the end of a region cuts reaches the viewer two
times, the same as an OSC or a DCS. The limit in README.md and in the module
doc names only OSC and DCS.

The fix is prose in two files. This round of the review loop is
record-only. A change to the two texts is new text that a review must read,
and that review can start a new fix round. The limit already gives the
behavior and its cause. The three other string sequences are rare in a
terminal recording. The APC of the kitty graphics protocol is the most
frequent of the three.

Boundary: this decision covers the words of the limit only. A defect in what
`finish()` does for these states reopens it.
