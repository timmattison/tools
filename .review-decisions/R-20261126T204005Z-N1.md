## `src/nle/src/ui/tests.rs` `a_second_recording_starts_the_chapter_panel_with_no_edit_and_no_search` — no assertion of empty fields after the second open

**Settled** 2026-11-26 (branch issue-164, finding R-20261126T204005Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. After the second open, the test asserts **Add
Chapter**, no **Save Chapter**, and the row of the second recording. It does
not assert that the Time field and the Label field are empty. The body of
issue #164 (PR #783, branch issue-164) says that the panel shows empty fields.

**The code gives empty fields.** `PropertiesPanel` gives the `serial` of the
document to `ChaptersPanel` as its key (`src/nle/src/ui.rs:295`). A new key
makes a new panel. The new panel starts `time_text` and `label_text` from
`String::new`, and `editing` from `None`. A panel that kept its signals keeps
`editing` too, and shows **Save Chapter**. Thus a mutant that removes the key
fails the test.

The test misses only a mutant that fills the two fields of a new panel from a
different source. No code of the branch does that.

**The fix costs more than the gap in this round.** The fix is two assertions in
the test file. This round of the review loop is record-only. A change of test
code starts one more review cycle.

**The boundary.** This record settles only the missing assertion of empty
fields after a keyed remount. Each of these occurrences reopens it:

- a change that removes the key, or that moves `time_text` or `label_text` out
  of `ChaptersPanel`
- a change that fills the two fields from the document or from a different
  signal when the panel starts
- a defect report of a field that keeps its text after a second open
