The finding is accurate. Lines 35-36 of the module doc say that the filter
rewrites the colon form in the semicolon form, with no condition.
`Converter::convert` returns `None` when the rules leave a color as it is, and
`Converter::rewrite` then keeps the text of the item. Thus `38:5:196` under
`cube keep`, and `38:2::1:2:3` under `truecolor keep`, stay in the colon form.

only. The behavior is correct: a color that no rule changes gives the same
screen as the recording with no filter, thus the filter makes no screen worse.
The bullet before it (lines 30-34) says "each new color", and `README.md` states
the same rule. The fix is a change of a doc comment, which is new text that a
review reads, and this round of the review loop is record-only.
