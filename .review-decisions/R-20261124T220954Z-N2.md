The finding is accurate. `LEFT_NOTICE` and `README.md:5341` say that the
selection moves to the oldest frame. `App::on_update` (`app.rs:165-175`)
selects `history.oldest()` (`history.rs:909-911`), which is the oldest entry.
That entry can be a gap, as `home_selects_the_oldest_entry` holds for `Home`.

be wrong when the oldest entry is a gap.

a gap tells the person what that entry is. Only the word "frame" in the
notice and in the README is too narrow. The row of `Home` in the key table
(`README.md:5324`) uses the same word for the same move.

The fix is prose in a constant and in the README. The round that found it
was record-only, and changed text is new text that the next review must
read. That review starts one more round, and the round costs more than one
word that the banner of the gap corrects on the screen.

The boundary: this settles the word "frame" for the oldest entry in
`LEFT_NOTICE`, in `README.md:5341`, and in the row of `Home`. A move that
selects an entry other than the oldest entry is a new finding.
