## `src/beta/NLE-ROADMAP.md` item 2 — the reason that the #154 filters are span filters stays as it is

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-154, finding R-20261127T052604Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. Item 2, **Filter interface**, says that the filters
of #154 are span filters "for the same reason" as time dilation: a region with
no event must also change its length. Only `pause-insertion` has that need.
`remove_pauses` keeps the length of a region with no event, and
`trim_silence` keeps it when the region has no input event and no output
event. The reason that all three filters share is the one that the sentence
gives for typing speed (#155): the event form cannot refuse a bad option.

The defect is one clause of a planning document. The contract in the same
item is correct: a span filter gets and gives a length, and it can give an
error. The code in `src/nle/src/pipeline.rs` and `src/nle/src/filters.rs`
holds the real rule, and the doc comment of `register_with_options` names
both causes of a refusal. A filter author who picks the wrong form gets the
correct answer from the contract, because an option that can be bad needs a
form that can give an error.

The fix costs more than the defect. A change of the roadmap is new text that
a fresh review must read, and the loop then starts one more fix round for one
clause of prose.

The boundary: this settles the reason that item 2 gives for the three filters
of #154. A statement in item 2 of the contract itself (the signature, what a
span filter gets or gives, or when the pipeline moves the later events) that
is wrong is a new finding. A wrong reason for a filter other than those of
#154 is a new finding too.
