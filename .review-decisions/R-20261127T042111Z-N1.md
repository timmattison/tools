## `src/ufa/README.md` — "because the controller answered at once" is false for a body that stalls

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch ufa, finding R-20261127T042111Z#N1)

The finding is correct. Since `fb9c909c9`, `broken_body` (http.rs:665) makes
`FailedAnswer::Unreadable` for a success status whose body closed or timed out
before its end. `delete_listed_vouchers` continues after such a voucher. A body
that stalls after the status thus costs the full 30-second request timeout
before the run continues. For that case, the reason in README.md:415-417 is
false.

The behavior is the one that the branch chose. A status arrived, thus the
controller is alive, and the next voucher can get an answer. A stalled body
costs one wait for that voucher, not one wait for each voucher after it, which
is the cost that the stop rule above (README.md:403-406) prevents. A fix changes
one sentence of the README. That change starts one more review cycle, and the
sentence gives a reason, not a step that a user obeys.

**The boundary.** A deletion run that stops after a success status, or that
makes each voucher after a stalled body wait the request timeout, is a new
occurrence. The words of the reason are settled.
