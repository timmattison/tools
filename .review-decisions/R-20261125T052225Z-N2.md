## `plugins/ic-inline/hooks/draw.tsx` — under `renderer` `image`, a terminal that draws no pixels shows the caption two times

**Settled** 2026-11-25 (branch ic-emit-rgba, finding R-20261125T052225Z#N2)

The finding is correct. In the pixel branch, the `Image` takes `alt={caption}`, and the `Text` under it shows the same caption. The engine draws the alt text in place of an `Image` on a terminal that draws no pixels (zellij, tmux, iTerm2, mosh). Thus such a terminal shows the caption two times.

The defect occurs only when a person sets `renderer` to `image` by hand on a terminal that draws no pixels. The default, `auto`, draws half blocks until the probe answers `yes`, thus such a terminal never gets the pixel branch under `auto`. The two lines hold the same text, thus the person loses no information.

The fix is not one obvious line. The probe gives its one-cell `Image` a blank `alt`, but no test of this repository shows what the engine draws for a blank `alt` in an `Image` of many rows. Removing the `Text` loses the caption on a terminal that draws pixels. Each shape is a change of behavior with a new test that draws the alt text. This round of the review loop is record-only. A change to `draw.tsx` is new code that the next review reads, and a new nit in it starts a new fix round. The cost of that review cycle is more than the cost of a repeated caption in a mode that a person must force by hand.

The boundary: this decision covers only the repeated caption of the pixel branch under a forced `renderer` of `image`. A repeated caption, or a lost caption, under `auto` or `blocks` is a new finding.
