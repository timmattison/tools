## `src/faulte/src/stream.rs` — "ends" names two moments of a sample

**Settled** 2026-11-24 (branch issue-669, finding R-20261124T174051Z#N4)

The finding is accurate. The module doc at `stream.rs:7` uses "ends" for the
moment when the parser completes the text of a sample: the `Processes:` line
of the next sample arrives. The doc of `HeaderStamps` (`stream.rs:517`) and the
doc of `ParsedEvent::Empty` (`stream.rs:44-46`) use "ends" for the end of the
time window of a sample, at its own header. STE asks for one word for one
meaning. Both uses date from round 1 (`a38039dd`, `d72c4147`).

The behavior is correct. The window of a sample comes from the stamps of its
own header and of the header before it. The tests
`a_finished_sample_states_its_window_and_the_time_that_it_ended` and
`a_sample_with_no_rows_is_refused_at_the_time_of_its_own_header` hold that
rule.

The fix is a change of wording in doc text. The round that found it was
record-only, and a change of doc text starts one more review round. That round
costs more than the defect, because each sentence names its own moment: the
sentence at `stream.rs:7` names the `Processes:` line of the next sample, and
the other two name the header of the sample itself.

The boundary: this settles the two uses of "ends" at these three doc sites. A
use of the word that a reader cannot resolve from its own sentence is a new
finding. A code path that takes the window of a sample from the wrong header is
a new finding, and a correctness defect.
