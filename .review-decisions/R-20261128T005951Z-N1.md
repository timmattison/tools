## `src/nle/src/pipeline.rs` `FilterRegistry::render` — rule 3 counts only the clips that play for the end of the splice

**Settled** 2026-11-28 (branch issue-157, finding R-20261128T005951Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. Rule 3 of the public doc of `FilterRegistry::render`
(`src/nle/src/pipeline.rs:446-449`) says that the end of the splice is "the
latest end of the part of a clip that plays". Rule 9 of the private `splice`
module (`src/nle/src/splice.rs:103-105`) also counts a clip that plays
nothing, at its start. The code obeys rule 9: `splice` sets
`end = end.max(part_end)` before the `continue`. For clip A from 0 s to 10 s
and clip Z with no length at 20 s, the render ends at 20 s, and
`EditProject::duration` gives 20 s. The first clause of rule 3 gives 10 s.
Thus rule 3 contradicts the sentence after it.

The defect is in the text only. The code and the tests are correct, and the
finding is not blocking.

**The fix costs more than the defect.** The fix is a change of one sentence of
a doc comment, and the same change in the PR body. This round of the
review-loop is record-only. A change of the doc is new text that a review must
read, and a fresh review can find a new nit in it. Then a new fix round
starts, and the loop does not end clean. The second sentence of rule 3 already
gives the correct end for a project with no overlap, `EditProject::duration`.

**The boundary.** This record settles only the words of rule 3 about the end
of the splice. These occurrences reopen it:

- a change of `splice` that makes a clip with no length move the end of the
  timeline differently from rule 9
- a test or a caller that reads rule 3 and expects the end of the clips that
  play only
- a change of the clause in rule 3 in a later round, which then must agree
  with rule 9
