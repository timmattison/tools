## `src/nle/src/filters/backspace.rs` — the doc of `probe_theme` gives the probe colors as the reason that equal cells look the same

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-165, finding R-20261127T201301Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. The doc of `probe_theme` (lines 403-409) ends with
"Thus two cells are equal only when a terminal in each theme draws them the
same." The sentence is true, but the probe colors are not its reason. Since
commit `99fca1c2`, `Cell` holds `fg_source` and `bg_source`, and
`TerminalState::eq` compares the grid. Thus two equal cells have equal sources,
and a terminal in each theme draws them the same. Commit `a9c4b84a` gives a
mutant with `TerminalTheme::auto()` in place of `probe_theme()`, and that
mutant passes each of the 34 tests of the filter.

The defect is in prose only. The filter compares two screens with
`TerminalState::eq`, and that comparison reads the sources. The test
`a_black_cell_is_not_a_blank_cell` holds the behavior.

**The fix costs more than the defect in this round.** The fix is prose: one
sentence of a doc comment. This round of the review loop is record-only. A
change is a diff that a review must read, and that review can find a new nit
in it. Then one more fix round starts.

**The boundary.** This record settles only the reason that the doc of
