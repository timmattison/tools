## `src/nle/src/editor/tests.rs` — no test of `Editor::export` exports a project with a chapter

**Settled** 2026-11-28 (branch issue-164, finding R-20261128T133436Z#N2)

The finding is accurate. The three tests of `Editor::export` use
`trimmed_editor` and `dilation_editor`, and the projects of both hold no
chapter. Thus `assert_eq!(written, editor.render())` compares two empty chapter
lists. No test in `src/nle/src/export.rs` or `src/nle/src/export/tests.rs`
names a chapter. A change that clears the chapters in `write_recording_file`,
or before the work file for beta, passes each test.

The two ends of the path have tests. The tests in
`src/nle/src/pipeline/tests.rs` hold the chapters of the render, for example
`with_no_filter_the_chapters_come_out_in_time_order_with_their_labels`. The
tests in `src/beta/src/export/web.rs` and `src/beta/src/export/video.rs` hold
the chapters of the web page and of the MP4 metadata. Between the two ends,
`export_recording_with` gives the `Recording` value whole to `Recording::save`,
and it does not read the chapters. Thus a drop of the chapters needs a change
that removes them on purpose.

The fix costs more than the defect. It is a new test, and the branch is in the
record-only round of the review loop. A new test is new code that a fresh
review reads, and a nit in it starts one more fix round.

The boundary: this record settles the projects with no chapter in the export
tests at the head `7b50133a`. These occurrences reopen it:

- a change in `src/nle/src/export.rs` or in `Editor::export` that reads,
  filters, or changes the chapters of the recording
- an export from the window that loses a chapter
- a test of the export path that names a chapter and asserts a wrong result
