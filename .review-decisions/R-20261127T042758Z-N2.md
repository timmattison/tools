## `src/ufa/src/commands/vouchers.rs` — a refused connection does not make the next voucher wait the timeout

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch ufa, finding R-20261127T042758Z#N2)

The finding is correct. The docs of `delete_vouchers_filtered`
(vouchers.rs:519-523) and `delete_listed_vouchers` (vouchers.rs:602-604) say
that each voucher after a failure with no status waits the full request
timeout. `FailedAnswer::Missing` holds a timeout and also a refused or reset
connection. A refused or reset connection fails at once, thus only a timeout
costs that wait.

The stop rule is correct for each case, because the controller gave no usable
answer. That first reason stays true. Only the second reason is too broad, and
README.md:403-405 gives it correctly, as the case of a controller that stops
answering. A fix changes two doc sentences. That change starts one more review
cycle and changes no behavior.

**The boundary.** A deletion that continues after a failure with no status, or
that stops after a failure with a status, is a new occurrence. The words of the
second reason are settled.
