## `Cargo.lock` — the `cookie`, `glib`, and `proc-macro-error` advisories that `msgs` brings

**Settled** 2026-11-24 (branch msgs, finding R-20261124T203119Z#I19)

The finding is accurate. Through `tauri`, the branch adds three `cargo audit`
findings that `main` does not have:

- RUSTSEC-2026-0594, `cookie` 0.18.2, through `tauri` and `tauri-runtime`.
  The defect needs a process that calls `fork()` and uses private cookies.
  `msgs` does neither.
- RUSTSEC-2024-0429, `glib` 0.18.5 (unsound), through the Linux GTK stack of
  `tauri`. `msgs` is for macOS only, thus that stack does not build.
- RUSTSEC-2024-0370, `proc-macro-error` (unmaintained), through the same
  Linux GTK stack.

**Tim decided to merge now, and to give the three findings to issue #739.**
The fix of the `cookie` finding is `cookie` 0.18.3, published
2026-11-23T07:45Z. It passes the 7-day cooldown on 2026-11-30, and a wait for
it holds the merge for six days for a defect that `msgs` cannot reach. The
comment
<https://github.com/timmattison/tools/issues/739#issuecomment-7149637889>
on issue #739 names the three findings, the reason for each one, and the date
of the cooldown.

**The boundary.** This record settles these three advisories only. A new
advisory in the `tauri` stack reopens it. A `msgs` change that calls `fork()`,
uses private cookies, or builds `msgs` for Linux also reopens it.
