**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-729, finding R-20261127T111429Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. Commit 24d0b3f87 added "or one of my places" to the
doc comment of `text`, and the edit left one line of about 100 characters,
then the short line "room that the width leaves." The other lines of the
paragraph wrap near 80 characters. `rustfmt` does not wrap comments, thus no
check finds the long line.

The defect is cosmetic. The words of the comment are correct, `rustdoc` joins
the lines into one paragraph, and no behavior changes.

The fix costs more than the defect. The branch is in a record-only round of
its review loop. A rewrap is a change that a fresh review reads, and that
review can find a new nit in the rewrapped text. That starts a new fix round
for the width of two lines of a comment.

The boundary: this decision settles the line width of the last paragraph of
the doc comment of `text` at `src/wdid/src/defs/render.rs:545-546`. A change
that edits the words of that paragraph rewraps it in the same commit. A long
line in a different comment is a new occurrence.
