## `src/wl/tests/cli.rs` — the module doc of the CLI tests keeps its sentence about ports that nothing listens on

**Settled** 2026-11-24 (branch wl, finding R-20261124T160606Z#N2)

The finding has two parts.

1. **The bind of port 1 is gone.** The red commit `1405edc3` replaced the test
   `privilege_note_when_no_process_is_named`, which held that bind, with
   `no_privilege_note_when_the_port_is_free`. The new test runs `wl` on port 1
   and binds no port. No test in `src/wl/tests/cli.rs` binds port 1 now. Thus
   a root run, or a container with `net.ipv4.ip_unprivileged_port_start=0`,
   cannot make `unheld_privileged_port_is_reported_free` fail. This part needs
   no change.
2. **The module doc is accurate in its purpose, but not in its words.** Lines
   3-5 say that the tests use only ports that nothing can listen on. But
   `no_privilege_note_when_a_process_is_named` listens on a port that the
   kernel picks (`127.0.0.1:0`). The isolation that the sentence promises is
   true: the two tests of port 1 bind no port, and no other process can get the
   port that the third test holds. The kernel never picks port 1 from its
   ephemeral range. A change of the sentence is a change of text only, and it
   starts a new review cycle on this branch for no change of behavior.

**The boundary.** This record settles the words of lines 3-5 of the module doc
only. A test in `src/wl/tests/cli.rs` that binds a fixed port, or that asks
about a port that a different test binds, is a new finding.
