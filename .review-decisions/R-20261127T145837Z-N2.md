## `src/nle/src/filters/viewport.rs` — the module doc says that the restore saves the position only

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-165, finding R-20261127T145837Z#N2)

The finding is accurate. The restore writes the pen (one SGR) before the CUP
and the `ESC 7` of the saved cursor. Thus the real terminal saves the pen of
the emulator at the end of the range, and a DECRC after the range gives that
pen, not the pen at the original `ESC 7`. The module doc (lines 74-75) says
that the restore saves the position only.

The spec, `specs/2026-11-27-nle-viewport-filter-design.md` (Known limits),
states the behavior exactly. The code obeys the spec. The tests that compare
the exact bytes of the restore (`src/nle/src/filters/viewport/tests.rs`, lines
507 and 1407) pin the SGR before the CUP and the `ESC 7`. Only the sentence of
the module doc is not exact.

**The fix costs more than the defect in this round.** The fix changes two lines
of a doc comment. This round of the review loop is record-only. A change is a
diff that a review must read, and that review can find a new nit in it. Then
one more fix round starts.

**The boundary.** This record settles only the sentence of the module doc about
the pen of `ESC 7`. A change of the order of the restore, or a restore that
gives a pen different from the pen that the spec states, is a new occurrence,
and it reopens this decision.
