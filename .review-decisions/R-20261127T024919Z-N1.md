## `src/nle/src/filters/backspace.rs` `erase_char` — under double type-ahead, the match path removes the inputs and keeps their echoes

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-152, finding R-20261127T024919Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. Under double type-ahead during a slow echo, the match
path of `erase_char` removes a typed key and the Backspace after it, but keeps
their echoes. An example is this order of events: in `h`, out `h`, in `r`, in
DEL, in `e`, out `r`, out `"\b \b"`, out `e`. The units of `r` and DEL have no
output, thus the screen after the DEL unit is the screen before `r`. The filter
removes the inputs `r` and DEL and cuts 0.55 s. The echoes `r` and `"\b \b"`
stay in the unit of `e`. Thus the viewer sees the typo for a moment, with no
key press. The screen at the end is correct.

The code and the docs agree. The module doc of `backspace.rs` (the third item
of "The limits") and the limits of the filter in `README.md` state this order
as a known limit. Tim decided to keep the limit as documented, and to change
no code.

**The fix costs more than the defect.** There are two possible fixes:

- A lookahead through `SpanContext` for a later echo of the pair. The filter
  must then read the output events after the unit, and match them to an
  earlier key. That is a new mechanism for a rare case, and the case needs two
  keys of type-ahead during a slow echo.
- Keep each Backspace unit with no output also on the match path. That makes
  the filter safe under type-ahead, but then a typo at a prompt that echoes
  nothing stays, and `with_no_echo_a_backspace_erases_the_newest_typed_key`
  changes. That cost falls on a common case to fix a rare one.

**The boundary.** This record settles only the case of double type-ahead
during a slow echo: a typed key and a Backspace whose units have no output
because their echoes come after a later key. These occurrences reopen it:

- the same symptom (an input removed, its echo kept) in a case that is not
  double type-ahead
- a change to the match path of `erase_char`, or to the limit text in
  `backspace.rs` or in `README.md`, that makes the code and the docs disagree
- a recording from real use in which this order is not rare
