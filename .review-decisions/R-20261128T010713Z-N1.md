**Settled** 2026-11-28 (branch issue-157, finding R-20261128T010713Z#N1)

The finding is accurate, and it states the same defect as
R-20261128T005951Z#N1 (`.review-decisions/R-20261128T005951Z-N1.md`). Rule 3
of the public doc of `FilterRegistry::render`
(`src/nle/src/pipeline.rs:447-449`) says that the end of the splice is "the
latest end of the part of a clip that plays". Rule 9 of the private `splice`
module (`src/nle/src/splice.rs:103-105`) and the code

10 s and clip Z with no length at 20 s, the render ends at 20 s, which is
`EditProject::duration`. The first clause of rule 3 gives 10 s. The section
"The splice" of the PR body repeats the same clause.

The defect is in the text only. The code and the tests are correct, and the
finding is not blocking.
