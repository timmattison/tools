## `src/nle/src/filters/keystroke_hud/paint.rs` — the CUP of the repair ignores origin mode

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-161, finding R-20261127T132506Z#N2)

The finding is correct. The repair writes an absolute CUP after the `ESC 8` of
the draw. A real terminal puts back DECOM with DECRC. Thus, for a program in
origin mode with a scroll region, the terminal counts that CUP from the top of
the region, and the cursor of the viewer goes to a different row than the
cursor of the replay. `termstate` has no DECOM, thus the two replays agree, and
no later repair corrects the viewer. The next absolute move of the program
puts the cursor back. The case needs origin mode, a scroll region, and a
repair after a draw.

The cause is the cause of R-20261127T014638Z#I2: `termstate` has no DECOM.
There are two fixes. A limit in README.md is prose, and it changes nothing that
the viewer shows. DECOM in `termstate` is a new mode in the emulator, with its
effect on each cursor move, on the scroll region, and on DECSC and DECRC. That
mechanism is too large for this rare case. Also this round of the review loop
is record-only, and each fix is new text or new code that a review must read.

Boundary: the reopen condition of R-20261127T014638Z#I2 also reopens this
decision: a `termstate` that models DECOM. A recording of a real program where
the cursor of the viewer stays on the wrong row after a repair reopens it too.
A new argument about origin mode does not.
