## `README.md` — the word "below" in the `idle-compression` entry of the list of nle filters

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-167, finding R-20261127T151842Z#N2)

The finding is accurate. The `idle-compression` entry of the list of filters in
the nle section says "The idle panel below gives its rules." The paragraph on
the idle panel ("The idle panel under the speed panel compresses idle time")
is above the list, and no paragraph after the list describes the idle filter.
The sibling entry of `redaction` says "above".

**The fix costs more than the defect in this round.** The entry names the idle
panel, and the nle section holds one paragraph on that panel. A reader who
looks below and finds nothing finds the paragraph some lines above it, in the
same section. The rules in that paragraph are correct, and no statement of a
rule, an option, or a default is wrong.

The fix is a change of one word in `README.md`. A review reads that change,
thus the fix starts one more review cycle. This round of the review loop is
record-only.

**The boundary.** This record settles only the direction word of this one
cross-reference. Each of these occurrences reopens it:

- a cross-reference that names a paragraph, a panel, or a heading that does
  not exist
- a statement in the entry or in the paragraph on the idle panel about an
  option, a default, or a rule that the code does not obey
- the same wrong direction in a different entry, when its target is not in the
  same section
