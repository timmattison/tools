## `src/nle/src/pipeline.rs` `FilterRegistry::apply` — a mark inside a region of an event or in-place filter can move by one step of an f64

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-167, finding R-20261127T022719Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. The split gives each mark inside the region the time
`mark.time - start`, and the join adds `start` again. The event arm and the
in-place arm give those marks back with no change, thus each one makes the
round trip. In f64, `(0.407 - 0.1) + 0.1` is `0.4069999999999999`, not
`0.407`. Step 9 of `FilterFn`, step 5 of `InPlaceFilterFn`, and
`src/beta/NLE-ROADMAP.md` say that such a mark keeps its time. The test
`a_mark_inside_or_after_the_region_of_an_in_place_filter_keeps_its_time` uses
2.0 s and 3.0 s, which make the round trip exactly.

**The fix costs more than the defect in this round.** The drift is at most one
step of an f64. The sum is monotonic, thus a mark inside the region stays at or
after the region start, and the stable sort keeps the order of the marks. The
three shipped filters (`cursor`, `idle_compression`, `time_dilation`) map a
relative time of 0 to 0. Thus a mark that drifts across the start of a later
range gets the same place within one step. The marks show on a strip in the
window, and a person cannot see one step of an f64 there.

The fix is small: the two arms keep the original time of each mark inside the
region, and a new test uses a range from 0.1 s and a mark at 0.407 s. But this
round of the review loop is record-only. A change of the code and a new test
start one more review cycle, and that review can find a new nit in the new
code.

**The boundary.** This record settles only the drift of one step of an f64 for
a mark inside the region of an event filter or an in-place filter. Each of
these occurrences reopens it:

- a drift of more than one step, or a drift of a mark that a span filter
  answers, or of a mark after the region
- a shipped filter that maps a relative time of 0 to a different place, for
  example pause insertion (#154) that puts a pause at the start of its range.
  Then a drift across the start of that range moves the mark by the length of
  the pause
- a place where a person reads a mark time at full precision, or a compare of
  a mark time for equality, for example in the chapter index (#164)
