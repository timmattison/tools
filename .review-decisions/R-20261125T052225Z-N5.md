## `plugins/ic-inline/hooks/picture.ts` — `DECODE_BOX` and `DECODE_SIDE` each state 720

**Settled** 2026-11-25 (branch ic-emit-rgba, finding R-20261125T052225Z#N5)

The finding is correct. `DECODE_BOX` gives `ic` the box `720x720`, and `DECODE_SIDE` is the largest side that `readEmitted` accepts. Each one states 720. An edit of one without the other makes `readEmitted` refuse a picture that `ic` prints, or accept a side that `ic` never prints.

The two constants are on adjacent lines, and the doc of `DECODE_SIDE` names `DECODE_BOX`, thus a person who edits one sees the other. The box cannot increase alone: the doc of `DECODE_BOX` says that the engine takes at most 2 MiB of pixels, and 720 by 720 by 4 bytes is just under that limit. A smaller box with an unchanged `DECODE_SIDE` gives a bound that is too wide, and it refuses no picture that `ic` prints.

The fix derives the box from the side, and it is a change of code. This round of the review loop is record-only. A change of code is new code that the next review reads, and a new nit in it starts a new fix round. The cost of that review cycle is more than the cost of two adjacent constants that a doc links.

The boundary: this decision covers only the two statements of 720 in `DECODE_BOX` and `DECODE_SIDE`. A third statement of the decode box in a different file, or a value of `DECODE_SIDE` that is smaller than a side of `DECODE_BOX`, is a new finding.
