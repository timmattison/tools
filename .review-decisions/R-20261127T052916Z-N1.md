## `README.md` `nle` filter list — the row of `cursor` names four of its five option keys

**Settled** 2026-11-27 (branch issue-154, finding R-20261127T052916Z#N1)

The finding is accurate. The row of `cursor` in the list of the filters of
`filters::registry()` gives `visibility`, `shape`, `blink` and `color`, but
not `burst_gap`. `CursorFile` accepts `burst_gap`
(`src/nle/src/filters/cursor.rs`), and each other row of the list gives every
key of its filter.

The defect is one word of prose, and the row itself points the reader to the
full table. The same row says that the section "The cursor filter" below
gives its options, and that table has a row for `burst_gap`, with its
default and its rule. A reader who writes a project file reads that table,
because the list does not give the values of the options either. The row
came in through a merge of `main`, and no code or test of this branch
depends on it.

The fix costs more than the defect. A change of `README.md` is new text that
a fresh review must read, and the loop then starts one more fix round for one
word.

The boundary: this settles the missing `burst_gap` in the row of `cursor`. A
row that names a key that its filter does not accept is a new finding. A
missing key in a row that does not point to a full table of its options is a
new finding too. The table of the cursor filter without a row for
`burst_gap` is a new finding.
