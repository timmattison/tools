## `src/nle/src/filters/typing_speed.rs` `from_options` — the jitter keeps its f64 default (second report)

**Settled** 2026-11-26 (branch issue-155, finding R-20261127T004416Z#N1)

This finding is the same claim as R-20261127T004114Z#N1, from a fresh review
of the same commits. The record of that finding,
`.review-decisions/R-20261127T004114Z-N1.md`, gives the full reason. This
finding gives no new argument and no new occurrence.

The finding is accurate. The jitter of `from_options` turns `Jitter::NONE`
into an `f64` and gives it to `Jitter::new` again at each call, but the pause
uses `DEFAULT_PAUSE` as it is. The finding itself states that the behavior is
correct, because `Jitter::new(0.0)` always succeeds.

The fix costs more than the defect. It is new code in `from_options`, a fresh
review must read it, and the review loop then starts one more fix round for a
change of shape that no test can see.

The boundary is the boundary of R-20261127T004114Z#N1: the shape of the jitter
default in `from_options`. A default that can fail its own constructor, or a
message for a bad jitter that does not name the option, is a new finding.
